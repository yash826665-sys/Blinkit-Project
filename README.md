# Blinkit-Project

# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Data Analysis & Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing Blinkit sales data to understand product performance, outlet characteristics, customer-facing metrics, and sales distribution across different outlet locations.

The project combines **Python-based exploratory data analysis and visualization** with an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to generate meaningful business insights.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a Blinkit-style grocery dataset containing **8,523 records and 12 attributes** related to products, outlets, sales, ratings, and outlet locations.

The analysis was performed using Python to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize the data. The results were then presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard for easier business interpretation.

### Key Questions Addressed

- What is the overall sales performance?
- Which item categories generate the highest sales?
- How does fat content affect sales?
- Which outlet locations generate the most revenue?
- How does outlet size relate to sales?
- How does outlet establishment year influence sales?
- Which outlet types perform best?
- How are sales distributed across different city tiers?

## 🧹 Data Cleaning

The dataset contained inconsistent values in the `Item Fat Content` column.

For example:

- `Low Fat`
- `low fat`
- `LF`
- `Regular`
- `reg`

These values were standardized into two consistent categories:

- **Low Fat**
- **Regular**

This was done using Pandas replacement operations before performing the analysis.

# 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

## 1. Sales by Fat Content

The analysis compares sales generated by products based on their fat content.

### Findings

- **Low Fat products:** 64.6% of total sales
- **Regular products:** 35.4% of total sales

Low Fat products generated approximately **$776K** in sales, while Regular products generated approximately **$425K**. 

---

## 2. Sales by Item Type

Sales were analyzed across different product categories.

The highest-performing categories include:

- Fruits and Vegetables
- Snack Foods
- Household
- Frozen Foods
- Dairy

According to the analysis, **Fruits and Vegetables** generated approximately **$178K**, followed by **Snack Foods** at approximately **$175K**. 

---

## 3. Fat Content Across City Tiers

Sales were compared across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 locations.

### Key Observation

Low Fat products generated higher sales than Regular products across all three tiers.

| City Tier | Low Fat Sales | Regular Sales |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | $215K | $121K |
| Tier 2 | $254K | $139K |
| Tier 3 | $307K | $165K |

Tier 3 locations generated the highest sales for both Low Fat and Regular products. 

---

## 4. Sales by Outlet Establishment Year

Sales were analyzed according to the year in which outlets were established.

The analysis shows variation in sales across establishment years, with some older and newer outlets producing significantly different sales levels.

The highest value shown in the analysis is approximately **$205K for 1998-established outlets**, while 2011 outlets recorded the lowest value at approximately **$78K**.

---

## 5. Sales by Outlet Size

Outlet sales were analyzed based on outlet size.

| Outlet Size | Share of Sales |
|---|---:|
| **Medium** | **42.3%** |
| **Small** | **37.0%** |
| **High** | **20.7%** |

Medium-sized outlets contributed the largest share of total sales. 


## 6. Sales by Outlet Location

Sales were compared across three outlet location tiers.

| Location | Total Sales |
|---|---:|
| **Tier 3** | **$472.13K** |
| **Tier 2** | **$393.15K** |
| **Tier 1** | **$336.40K** |

Tier 3 locations generated the highest total sales, followed by Tier 2 and Tier 1 locations.
